`ruleDocs`: for each documented rule, why it exists (linking the OKF
specification) and an example that breaks it, with the findings it produces
and the fix, tested so they stay true. The rule pages at
https://www.okforest.ai/docs/rules/ show them. Five rules so far: `okf/type`,
`okf/title`, `okf/broken-link`, `okf/index-entries`, and `okf/verified`.
