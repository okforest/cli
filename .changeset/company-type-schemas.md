`okforest init --template company` writes a [type schema](https://www.okforest.ai/docs/types/) for each of its two types, `Organization` and `Customer Profile`, in `.okforest/types/`: the fields each can carry (a website, the year founded, and headquarters; industries, company size, regions, and the buyer's role), shown in the OKForest App and to AI assistants. None is required, and the starter concepts list them, commented out, to fill in.
